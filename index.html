About Me

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior, born 5 February 1992 in Mogi das Cruzes, Brazil, is the imagined 60-year-old of this portfolio. After a playing career that started at Santos, he studies coaching and sports management and becomes head coach of the Brazil national team.

In this future version of his story he is retiring in 2052 after leading Brazil with creative, attacking and disciplined football.

“Play with joy, defend with discipline.”

— Imagined motto of the Brazil team, 2047–2052

Fans often call him by the nickname Ney, and joga bonito (“play beautifully”) is the spirit of his teams.

Education
